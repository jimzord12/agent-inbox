## Description

**Goal:** crashes, lost answers and expired leases never cause a false delivery or a repeated prompt (spec section 7 and 13, level C).
**Exit condition:** fault tests at every phase boundary pass; reconciliation, orphan-tab discovery and availability tracking work; spec section 15 'Crash and storage recovery' lines are met.
